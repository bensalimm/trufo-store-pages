Trufo - True or False Quiz Game

About Trufo

Trufo is an engaging true or false quiz game that tests your knowledge across multiple categories including General Knowledge, Science, Geography, History, Technology, and Fun Facts. The app supports English, Arabic, and French languages.

How It Works

  • Select a category or choose random questions
  • Answer true or false questions within the time limit
  • Earn points for correct answers
  • Track your statistics and achievements
  • Upgrade to Premium for lifetime access

Common Issues

App Crashes on Launch

If the app crashes when you open it, try restarting your device. If the problem persists, ensure you have the latest version installed from the App Store.

Purchase Not Working

If you're having trouble with in-app purchases, make sure you're signed in to your Apple ID and have a stable internet connection. If you've already purchased Premium but it's not showing, try using the "Restore Purchase" option in the settings.

Game Not Saving Progress

Game statistics and settings are stored locally on your device. If data is not saving, check that you have sufficient storage space available on your device.

Sound Not Playing

Check that your device volume is turned on and not muted. You can also check the sound settings within the app to ensure sound effects are enabled.
