Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2024

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Trufo handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information.

Data Collection

Trufo does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information from users. Specifically:

  • No Advertisements: The app does not display advertisements or use any ad networks.
  • No Analytics: The app does not use analytics services or tracking technologies.
  • No User Accounts: The app does not require account creation, login, or registration.
  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or location data.

Local Data Storage

The app stores game-related data locally on your device using device storage. This includes:

  • Game statistics (best score, games played, accuracy)
  • App settings (sound preferences, language selection)
  • Premium purchase status
  • Achievement progress

This data is stored only on your device and is not transmitted to any external servers or third parties. You can clear this data at any time through the app's settings.

In-App Purchases

Trufo offers a premium lifetime access purchase through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Payment processing is handled entirely by Apple App Store. We do not collect or store any payment information.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. We do not transfer your data to external parties for marketing or any other purposes.

Data Security

Since we do not collect personal data transmitted to external servers, there is minimal security risk. Local data stored on your device is protected by your device's security features.

Children's Privacy

Trufo is suitable for users of all ages. Since we do not collect personal information, we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access local app data through your device settings
  • Delete app data by uninstalling the app or using the clear data option in app settings
  • Disable local storage through your device settings

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in this document and updated "Last Updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through our support page.
